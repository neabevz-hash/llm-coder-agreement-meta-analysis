Run 2026-10-23 11:24 UTC. Backward and forward citations of every eligible full-text report, the five syntheses named in the protocol and two further reviews, via OpenAlex (references and cited-by) and Semantic Scholar (reference lists). New unique records: 9,248; removed automatically by the same rules as S4.4: 9,023; screened: 225 (search/runs/2026-10-23-citations/).

#### S4.7 AI tools
